------------------------------------------------------------
Cosmic Explorer Sensitivity Curves
------------------------------------------------------------

Strain ASDs:
* cosmic_explorer_strain.txt: the baseline 40 km detector
* cosmic_explorer_20km_strain.txt: the baseline 20 km detector

Displacement ASDs:
* cosmic_explorer_displacement.txt: the baseline 40 km detector
* cosmic_explorer_20km_displacement.txt: the baseline 20 km detector

Strain curves are calculated for a source 15 deg off of normal incidence. Note
that, due to the long arms of Cosmic Explorer, displacement cannot be converted
into strain by simply dividing by the arm length.
